## Must-fix (most important first)

1. **The same student's data contradicts itself across modules.** Flight Deck credentials (AAS Medical Assisting, Riverbend 2019 + PM certificate; 01) vs Profile prior credentials (Associate in Business Administration, Collège Ahuntsic 2017; 20). Staff panel preferred name "Amy", WhatsApp, a pending surname correction and contact phones +1 514… (26) vs student Profile "None set", Email, no pending correction, +1 555 010 0000 (20). *Change:* drive every screen from one fixture per student (and one source in production), and add a cross-screen consistency test for name, credentials, contacts and corrections.
2. **The staff surfaces aren't in the brand system.** The support desk and the lower workspace are generic grey/blue with a sans H1 and blue CTA (14/15/16/26). *Change:* reuse the student shell tokens (parchment surfaces, serif H1, gold eyebrow, dark-ink primary button, same sidebar pattern and grouping labels). Remove the "END OF PROFILE & IDENTITY" seam, and apply one container style to every workspace section.
3. **Raw system values are shown to staff.** `waiting_on_student`, `in_progress`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `hs_transcript`, ISO timestamps, "Uploaded Not provided", "Version Not provided", "Fingerprint unavailable" (26/27/28). *Change:* map them to the same human labels the desk uses, format dates as "Oct 25, 2026 · 9:00", hide empty metadata, and turn the snapshot/fingerprint block into a single plain-language notice.
4. **The support desk state is contradictory.** The card says "Assigned: Camille Moreau" but the dropdown says "Unassigned". The "Needs response" filter is selected but all 5 tickets show, including resolved ones. "Unassigned 2" vs 1 shown. "Overdue 2" has no overdue marker. Ages are "1 day" for tickets opened 12 days or 2 months ago. Three vocabularies (New / open / Received) describe one state (14/16/26/09). *Change:* fix the assignment binding and the filter default, make the KPI tiles clickable filters that match the list, add overdue markers, compute age from the open date, and publish one staff↔student status map.
5. **Student state feedback is wrong or incomplete.** (a) The toast says "you reported Challenge 3 done" while the select still shows "Started / Overdue since Oct 22" (22), and the metric that should check this measured a zero rect. (b) The Profile glance says "Nothing for you to do" while a guardian consent upload is requested (20), and in s09 01 the residency resubmission and Jordan Lake consent are missing from it. *Change:* update the control optimistically (or show the toast only after the update), and feed every student-actionable item (consent uploads, resubmissions) into "Needs your attention".
6. **The dialog footer overlaps content.** In the correction and add-contact dialogs, content renders below the sticky footer (s09 07/08/09, s10 25). *Change:* make the body the only scroll region (flex column, footer outside the scroll area) and put the info notice above the footer.
7. **The workspace layout contradicts itself.** "Needs a decision (2)" is directly followed by "Needs attention 0 items" and "No canonical credit records" for a student with 63 credits. Tabs don't scope content. There's "Back to Students" with no Students nav. The sidebar is "Student Portal" on one page and "Admin Portal" on the other (14/26). *Change:* merge the decision and attention queues, make the tabs actually switch panels, add a Students nav item, and unify the subtitle.
8. **Mobile length and density on the student side.** Flight Deck is 5150px and Roadmap 5587px at 390. Credits are repeated three times. Tracker labels are about 9px (02/06/22). *Change:* drop the duplicate credit summaries (keep one), collapse non-current course cards by default at 390, and swap the 6-label tracker for "Step 2 of 6: Started" plus dots on narrow widths.

## Nice-to-haves

- Profile header at 390: put the avatar inline with the name, move the "PNG, JPEG…" hint into the upload flow, and make the section chips a single horizontally scrolling row (21, s09 10).
- Unify "Verified" chip styling (filled black on 01 vs gold outline on 17/20) and use sentence case across the board ("Pending review", "Contact information").
- Documents: use warm tab styling (17/19), list the files before the upload zone or beside it at 1440, use a 2×2 or single-row stat layout at 390 (18), and use the same accepted-types list as Support.
- Support: explain "Received" vs "With your advisor", or merge them. Put "Attach a file" inside the reply composer (10/13).
- Staff 768: collapse the sidebar to icons and fix the clipped snapshot tile (28).
- Replace the "Awaiting decision ↑" pill with an explicit "Review request" link (26).
- Fix "Academic records does not apply" → "do not apply"; rename "Fix" to "Upload new file" (s09 01).
- Flight Deck legend: explain why a "Complete" phase shows one accepted and one provider-complete bar (01).

## Verdict

**Two portals, not one:** the student side reads as one cohesive, branded portal (Flight Deck, Roadmap, Support, Documents and Profile share a shell, type, palette and voice, with Documents the weakest). The staff side is a separate generic admin app with a branded Profile island bolted in. Because the two sides disagree about the same student's facts and statuses, the product as a whole does not yet read as one cohesive portal. It doesn't meet a 9/10 bar on any pillar.
