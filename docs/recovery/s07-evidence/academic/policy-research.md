# S07 plan-review policy research

Retrieved 2026-10-26 via web search/fetch from official sources only. Purpose: decide how strongly each plan-review rule (`shared/student-roadmap-plan-review.ts`) may be worded. **None of this is adopted Lumière policy and none of it is legal or academic advice.** All rules stay `warning` severity (staff must acknowledge before publish); none is blocking.

Design stance: rules are **data-driven on staff-entered fields** (term dates, gate evidence, equivalency status, credit use). No credit threshold, GPA or window length is encoded. The only constant is `STALE_SCHEDULE_DAYS = 21`, a heuristic about the plan's own dates, not an institutional rule.

## Not confirmed / out of scope
- Whether a "12-week term" or specific term dates apply to a given plan: take them from the Academic Calendar per term; staff enter them.
- Any GPA/credit gate for graduate-level capstones or specific accounting programs.
- Whether TESU's duplication-of-credit policy blocks a given pair of courses.
- Effective dates and transition rules of the Washington October 2025 rule amendments.
- The ten-case PowerEd benchmark was not loaded or referenced.

## Severity decision
All codes are `warning`. Reasons: (1) the plan's dates are staff-entered, so a mismatch may be a data error rather than a policy breach; (2) the institutional facts above are catalog-year dependent; (3) a blocking rule would stop publication on the strength of unapproved research. Messages state which data triggered them and what to confirm.
