> **INTERNAL STAFF RECORD.** This file names the awarding school (Penn Foster High School).
> Do not give it to the student or family before disclosure is authorized.

- **Sources:** accessed 2026-10-27. Source IDs (CAT, WEB-CURR) are defined in `CORE-CURRICULUM-MATRIX.md`.
- **Requirement:** 5 elective credits = 10 half-units (CAT "General Curriculum Requirements"). This is a credit requirement, not a course count.

## How to read the availability columns

- **CAT list:** the title appears in the CAT section "Elective selections include".
- **WEB list:** the title appears under "Electives" on WEB-CURR.
- **Availability:**
  - **Listed (both):** the strongest public evidence. The school must still confirm it for this student.
  - **Listed (one source):** treat with caution.
  - **UNCONFIRMED:** the course is described in the catalog but appears in neither elective list.

**No elective is confirmed as available to this student until the school says so in writing.**

## 5. Proposed initial selection (RECOMMENDATION ONLY)

- **Status:** a suggestion. It is not school approval and not a student selection.
- **Before staff enter it in the Portal:**
  - the owner approves it, **and**
  - the school confirms each course's availability for this student (Q7).
- **Assumptions:** a new standard-diploma student, no transfer credit, and English is not the student's first language.

| # | Course (CAT title) | Credit | Half-units | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Algebra I | 1 | 2 | The core math (General Math I/II, Personal Finance) is foundational. Algebra I is the course universities most often look for, and it opens Geometry, Algebra II and Chemistry later if needed. No prerequisite. |
| 2 | English Communication | 1 | 2 | Grammar, word choice and writing. It directly supports four required English courses for a non-native speaker. No prerequisite. |
| 3 | Psychology | 1 | 2 | A broad social-science course with general interest and no prerequisite. |
| 4 | Spanish | 1 | 2 | A world language with practical value in Morocco. Builds on existing multilingual skill (Arabic/French are common). No prerequisite. |
| 5 | Microsoft Word and Excel | 1 | 2 | A practical digital skill, useful in every later course and job. No prerequisite. Office is already required by the school. |
| | **Total** | **5** | **10** | |

**Why these five:**
- All five appear on **both** the CAT elective list and WEB-CURR, are 1 credit each, and have **no prerequisite**.
- They cover math, language, social science and digital skills.
- None is a pathway-restricted course.

**Sequencing suggestion:**
- Schedule English Communication early, alongside English 1–2.
- Take Algebra I after General Math I.
- The other three are flexible fillers.

**Alternates if the school declines one or the student prefers:**
- Geometry (take after Algebra I; good for a STEM-minded student).
- Music Appreciation or Art Appreciation (arts breadth).
- General Science.
- American Literature (heavy reading; better for a strong English reader).
- Small Business Management (practical).

**Avoid:**
- Algebra II or Chemistry before Algebra I.
- Economics, until the school confirms how it differs from the core U.S. Government and Economics.
- Pathway-only courses.
- Any title from §4.
